# M4 Kindle Checklist

**NOT APPLICABLE.**

M4 implements and verifies pure Kotlin game-history and undo semantics. It has
no Android UI, input, lifecycle, storage, networking, display, or
device-specific acceptance criterion. `adb devices -l` discovered the preferred
Kindle (`G090MJ0574130HMR`, `KFDOWI`) and an emulator (`emulator-5554`), but
device interaction would not add M4 evidence and could disturb existing
installations or data. Neither device was modified.
