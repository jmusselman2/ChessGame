# M3 Kindle Checklist

**NOT APPLICABLE.**

M3 implements and verifies the pure Kotlin legal-move and draw-rule engine. It
has no Android UI, input, lifecycle, storage, networking, display, or
device-specific acceptance criterion. `adb devices -l` discovered the preferred
Kindle (`G090MJ0574130HMR`, `KFDOWI`) and an emulator (`emulator-5554`), but
installing or launching the app would not add evidence for M3 and could disturb
existing installations or data. Neither device was modified.
