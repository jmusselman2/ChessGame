# M2 Kindle Checklist

**NOT APPLICABLE.**

M2 defines and verifies pure Kotlin chess-domain values and the standard initial
position. It has no Android UI, input, lifecycle, storage, networking, display,
or device-specific acceptance criterion. The preferred Kindle was nevertheless
discovered with `adb devices -l` as serial `G090MJ0574130HMR`, model `KFDOWI`.
Installing or launching the app would not provide evidence for M2 and was
therefore avoided to protect the existing device installation and data.
